Covert Reaction

Sid Meier's Covert Action (1990), reimplemented.

Covert Reaction plays with the files of your own copy of the original game (GOG or the floppies): the folder with TEXT.DTA, TITLE2.PAN, FACES.PIC and the rest.

or drop the folder (or its files) here

Your game files are stored in this browser.

The files are copied into this browser's storage (IndexedDB) and never leave your computer. Saved games stay here too. Alt+Enter toggles full screen.
